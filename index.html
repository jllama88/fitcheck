    fitcheck

    Your closet, styled. FitCheck is a character creator for the clothes you already own: photograph them once, see them on your avatar, then cycle, match and save looks in seconds.

    Idea

    Most people wear a fraction of what they own. FitCheck turns the closet into an inventory you can play with: every real piece becomes a swappable slot, the way a game's dressing room works. It suggests, you decide.

    Promise: Get more outfits out of what you already have. Audience: people who stand at the closet every morning and still feel they have nothing to wear.

    Logo

    A hanger with a check in it. The hook reads as "closet", the check reads as "approved". It works at 16px and as an app icon.
